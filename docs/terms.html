Terms — Draft

A concise product-policy draft for pilots. This is not a final legal instrument and requires counsel review before commercial launch.

Status: working draft only. Jurisdiction, consumer-rights language, warranty terms, liability limitations, and provider details must be reviewed before production publication.

Core service terms

  • LocalProof provides trust, destination information, QR experiences, operator tools, and offline infrastructure.
  • A LocalProof account, QR, or device does not itself mean a guide is professionally certified.
  • Certification may rely on OpenProof or other approved issuers.
  • Payment may cover software, hardware, or verification processing but does not guarantee certification.
  • Destination data can become stale; users should confirm time-sensitive safety, transport, regulatory, and health information.
  • Hardware may be sold, loaned, sponsored, or provided under earn-to-own terms.
  • Users must not clone identities, manipulate rewards, impersonate credentials, or intentionally distribute false safety information.

Commercial agreements

Enterprise deployments, hardware warranties, service levels, taxes, cancellation rights, and sponsor obligations should be documented separately in market-specific agreements.
