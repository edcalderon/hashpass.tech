# HASHPASS product context

HASHPASS connects events, organizers, speakers and attendees. People discover
upcoming events, keep access passes, arrange meetings and use community features.
The product runs on web and native mobile, supports light/dark themes, and uses
English, Spanish and Korean interface copy.

Public landing and event discovery should help visitors understand the service
and find real upcoming events without signing in. Authenticated views manage
passes, rewards and account data. Event catalogs and organizer media supply truth;
no synthetic balances or invented event listings belong in the interface.

The non-custodial wallet is in development. Client-side keys, encrypted backups,
and enrollment/onboarding are implemented locally. Production wallet creation,
chain reads, signing, external recovery compatibility and hardware integrations
are gated or pending. Do not imply those features are live or audited.

The visual direction and reusable UI contracts live in DESIGN.md. Preserve
accessibility, reduced-motion behavior, device-local wallet secrets, and canonical
account isolation through all design changes.
