# LocalPass commercial model

LocalPass is designed as a **physical-to-digital destination network**, not merely a travel app.

The product family has three layers:

1. **LocalPass Platform** — destination packs, discovery, offline experiences, identity, analytics and operator tooling.
2. **LocalPass Node** — fixed offline distribution point for venues and public tourism infrastructure.
3. **LocalPass Guide** — wearable, dynamic-QR companion for human guides and mobile tourism operators.

The commercial goal is to make useful local information free or very low-friction for travelers while charging for infrastructure, premium operator services, destination management, verification workflows and managed deployments.

## Commercial principles

- Traveler access should remain free for the core guide experience.
- The QR compatibility layer should be open enough to maximize adoption.
- **Certification is earned from evidence; it is never bought.**
- OpenProof can be an initial trusted issuer, but LocalPass must support a registry of approved credential issuers.
- Hardware is optional. LocalPass accounts and basic QR links can exist without buying a device.
- Pilot hardware can be sponsored, loaned or earned-to-own; mature deployments should not rely on permanent free hardware.
- The best long-term buyer may be a destination organization, municipality, hotel group or tour operator rather than an individual guide.
