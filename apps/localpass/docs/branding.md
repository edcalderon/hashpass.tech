# LocalPass brand system

LocalPass is the global product brand. **LocalPass Node** is the hardware companion sub-brand.

## Brand idea

LocalPass should feel like a local welcome rather than infrastructure.

The mark combines:

- a destination / place silhouette;
- landscape and movement;
- flowing water / pathways;
- a small warm sun;
- deep teal for trust and resilience;
- aqua for discovery and movement;
- gold for warmth and local hospitality.

## Primary assets

- `/brand/localpass-mark.svg` — compact global mark for app icons, favicon, QR signage and hardware faceplates.
- `/brand/localpass-logo.svg` — horizontal LocalPass wordmark lockup.

## Product naming

Use:

- **LocalPass** — global project / platform
- **LocalPass Node** — offline hardware distribution companion
- **LocalPass Guatapé** — destination deployment / destination pack

Avoid making “AI” part of the master brand name. AI is a capability; LocalPass is the user-facing brand.

## Colors

- Deep teal: `#06384A`
- Aqua: `#1BA6A8`
- Secondary teal: `#0B5E70`
- Golden sun: `#FDBA2D`
- Warm cream: `#F7F2E8`

## Brand promise

> Local places. Brighter journeys.

For hackathon messaging, the product proposition is:

> Offline tourism intelligence for real-world destinations.

## Hardware sub-brand

LocalPass Node uses the same mark and palette but can add material cues such as bamboo / wood, warm stone-like recycled polymer, and a subtle amber status light. The node should feel appropriate on a hostel desk, café counter, tourist office or transport terminal—not like networking equipment.
