# Development impact

Small operators are often absent when discovery depends on global platforms, paid placement, continuous connectivity, and maintained websites. LocalPass packages their essential discovery information into a compact public-good layer. The itinerary score deliberately favors relevant local operators while keeping constraints and reasons visible to the traveler.

The approach can be stewarded by a destination organization: curate a signed compact pack while connected, distribute it over inexpensive bandwidth, and let visitors reuse it without further data cost. This prototype measures coverage and response characteristics, but does not claim causality or business-income gains without a field pilot.
