Accessed 3 October 2026.

- [Colombia Travel — Things to do in lakeside Guatapé](https://colombia.travel/en/encanto/discover-watersports-hiking-and-art-in-lakeside-guatape): English-language destination, reservoir, and Piedra del Peñol context.
- [Medellín Mayor's Office — Terminal travel](https://www.medellin.gov.co/es/sala-de-prensa/noticias/desde-terminales-medellin-se-viven-experiencias-de-viaje-que-dejan-huella/): Terminal del Norte context and Guatapé as an intermunicipal destination.
- Colombia's national emergency number `123` should be reconfirmed against current government travel advice before a production launch.

Named micro-businesses in the prototype are clearly presented as **demo listings**, not verified commercial claims. Prices, hours, coordinates, payment methods, operator availability, and travel duration are plausible prototype estimates and must be verified locally. LocalPass is not exhaustive and does not replace official emergency, medical, weather, or transport information.
