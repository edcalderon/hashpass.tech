# Challenge fit

LocalPass answers the World Bank / Small AI for Development prompt with the smallest useful AI-like runtime that keeps creating value after connectivity disappears. Instead of a generic chatbot, it combines curated knowledge, local intent parsing, retrieval, and deterministic planning.

The economic inclusion mechanism is explicit: locally owned listings receive a transparent scoring boost and a visible **Support local** marker. This gives micro-businesses a discovery channel without requiring constant connectivity or sophisticated infrastructure.
