# Discovery and campaign city-media attribution

The `HashpassDiscoveryCoverLoop` and BSL Bogotá welcome use real city footage
and photography as geographic context. They do not represent the footage as a
specific Hashpass venue or event. The dashboard's copy and event data remain
the source of truth.
