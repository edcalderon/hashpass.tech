# CLF 2026 visual identity

## Style prompt

Cripto Latin Fest 2026 is an energetic, future-facing Latin American event identity: cobalt light moving across a midnight-blue field, with real community footage kept tactile and human beneath precise technical overlays. The visual should feel like a credible public event broadcast, never like a generic crypto dashboard.

## Colors

- `#046BD2` — CLF cobalt, primary signal
- `#06111F` — midnight, base canvas
- `#8FD3FF` — electric sky, highlight
- `#FFFFFF` — display type
- `#B8CAE0` — supporting metadata

## Typography

- Space Grotesk — display and event statements
- IBM Plex Mono — compact metadata and the CLF badge

## What not to do

- Do not use Bitcoin-gold gradients or neon green.
- Do not show archived 2025 dates or editions.
- Do not let title overlays obscure the people in the footage.
- Do not introduce generic corporate stock imagery.
