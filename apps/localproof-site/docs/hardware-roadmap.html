Hardware Roadmap

LocalProof hardware should preserve a warm tourism aesthetic while progressively standardizing electronics, enclosures, provisioning, and field operations.

LocalProof Guide

The wearable guide concept should use e-ink because the QR remains readable with very low power consumption between refreshes.

  • ESP32-class controller;
  • 2–3 inch class e-ink display;
  • Wi‑Fi + BLE;
  • rechargeable battery and USB‑C;
  • unique device identity;
  • optional secure key storage;
  • lanyard / clip enclosure;
  • simple status LED or haptic feedback.

Manufacturing rule

Wood is the brand skin, not the structural dependency. Use thin bamboo/wood veneer or interchangeable destination faceplates over a universal internal chassis.
