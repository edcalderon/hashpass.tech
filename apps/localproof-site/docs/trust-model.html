ISSUERS, PROVENANCE & REVOCATION

Trust Model

LocalProof is designed to make trust inspectable. Device ownership, identity verification, professional certification, and paid services are deliberately separated.

What can be proven

  • who issued a credential;
  • what type of credential it is;
  • who it applies to;
  • its jurisdiction;
  • when it was issued;
  • whether it is current, expired, or revoked.

Issuer registry

Approved issuers may include OpenProof, municipalities, tourism authorities, accredited operators, parks authorities, associations, universities, or other vetted organizations.

Never conflate

SignalWhat it means
Owns a LocalProof devicePossesses compatible hardware only.
Verified profileIdentity/profile evidence was checked.
Certified guideProfessional credential from an approved issuer.
Sponsored placementPaid/promotional visibility, not trust certification.

Revocation

Credentials and hardware identity must be independently revocable. A genuine device should never permanently preserve a guide's trusted status after the professional credential itself has expired or been revoked.
