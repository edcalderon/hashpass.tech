TRUSTED LOCAL PLACES

Venues

Venues can host LocalProof as a useful traveler service while gaining visibility, operational feedback, and participation rewards—without turning trust into a pay-to-play badge.

Who can participate

Hotels, hostels, cafés, restaurants, attractions, tourism offices, transport terminals, cultural institutions, and community businesses can participate.

Commercial model

LayerSuggested pilot approach
Basic listing / QRFree or sponsor-funded.
Local Node hardwarePurchase, loan, earn-to-own, or sponsor-funded.
Venue software/serviceRecurring subscription hypothesis after pilots.
Destination-wide deploymentB2B contract with DMO, municipality, association, or sponsor.

Operator rewards

Reward actions that improve destination quality, not vanity engagement.

  • keep opening hours and contact data current;
  • report closures, route changes, or outdated information;
  • maintain healthy Local Nodes;
  • add verified local events or useful places;
  • translate and improve destination content;
  • respond to legitimate traveler feedback.

Economic rewards

Points may unlock service credits, hardware discounts, partner benefits, editorial features, destination-campaign eligibility, and advanced analytics. Sponsored placement must remain distinguishable from trust or certification.
