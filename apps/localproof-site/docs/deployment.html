Destination Deployment

LocalProof should launch destination by destination, with enough density that a traveler encounters the network repeatedly during a real trip.

Recommended first pilot density

AssetTarget
Venue Nodes10–20
Active Guides10–30
Destination pack1 maintained source of truth
Issuer / tourism partner1+

Phase 1 — design partners

Recruit guides, hostels/hotels, cafés, attractions, tourism offices, transport operators, and local businesses.

Phase 2 — sponsor

Find a destination-wide buyer such as a municipality, DMO, chamber of commerce, hotel group, association, or ecosystem sponsor.

Phase 3 — repeatable kit

Package destination onboarding, packs, Nodes, Guide devices, operator dashboards, issuer integration, analytics, and support into a repeatable deployment service.

What to measure

  • active device rate;
  • 30/90-day operator retention;
  • scans per active Node/Guide;
  • content freshness;
  • verified-operator participation;
  • traveler-to-local-business discovery;
  • support and replacement rates;
  • sponsor renewal intent.
