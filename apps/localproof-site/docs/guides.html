VERIFICATION & PORTABLE TRUST

Guides

A guide can participate without buying hardware. Verification and professional certification are separate layers, and dynamic QR experiences can follow the guide from one attraction to the next.

Participation levels

LevelMeaningTypical capabilities
CommunityBasic LocalProof profile. No professional-certification claim.Profile, basic QR, destination access.
Verified GuideIdentity/profile evidence checked.Dynamic QR, visible verification status, hardware-program eligibility.
Certified / Trusted LocalProfessional credential from an approved issuer.Credential provenance, official-program eligibility, stronger trust presentation.
Core rule: paying for verification processing, software, or hardware never guarantees certification.

Portable Guide concept

LocalProof Guide is a wearable, tamagotchi-like badge concept with an e-ink display. The guide chooses the active attraction or route, and the screen refreshes to show the relevant QR and context.

Why dynamic QR

  • One guide identity can cover many stops.
  • No reprinting for each attraction.
  • QR can switch between online resolver and local/offline mode.
  • Device identity and guide credential are separate, so either can be revoked independently.

Issuer independence

OpenProof may be an initial issuer, but LocalProof is designed for a registry of approved issuers including municipalities, tourism authorities, associations, accredited operators, parks authorities, universities, and training institutions.
