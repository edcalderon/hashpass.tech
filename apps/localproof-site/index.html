WHAT LOCALPROOF IS

A trust layer, not a closed marketplace.

LocalProof separates identity, professional credentials, venue trust, and device ownership. A guide does not become “certified” because they paid for hardware, and a venue does not become trusted because it purchased placement.

OpenProof can be an initial credential issuer, while municipalities, tourism authorities, associations, training institutions, and other approved organizations can participate through a common issuer model.

LOCAL NODES

Offline access where mobile data fails.

A Local Node is a small destination access point that distributes a lightweight local guide and destination pack over nearby Wi‑Fi. The node is infrastructure—not the AI brain.

  • Local Wi‑Fi / captive portal
  • Destination pack distribution
  • Essential information fallback
  • Health and freshness status
  • Works with fixed venues or temporary deployments

HARDWARE ROADMAP

Prototype beautifully. Manufacture simply.

The industrial strategy keeps the visible product warm and destination-friendly while standardizing the electronics underneath.

01Hackathon / prototype

3D-printed shell, off-the-shelf boards, laser-cut bamboo accents, simple captive portal.

0220–100 unit pilot

Custom PCB, small-run molded shell, replaceable faceplate, field analytics, deployment playbook.

03100–1,000 units

Standardized enclosure, production e-ink Guide device, controlled provisioning, repeatable destination kits.

041,000+ deployment

Injection molding, regulatory certification by target market, secure device identity, managed fleet operations.
