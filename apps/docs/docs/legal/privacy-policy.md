# HASHPASS Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 25 September 2026<br />
**Controller:** HASHPASS TECHNOLOGIES OÜ (Registry Code 17603547)<br />
**Registered office:** Narva mnt 5, Tallinn, Estonia<br />
**Privacy contact:** [privacy@hashpass.tech](mailto:privacy@hashpass.tech)

This Privacy Policy explains how HASHPASS TECHNOLOGIES OÜ ("HASHPASS",
"we", "us", or "our") processes personal data when you use HASHPASS
websites, applications, event pages, passes, credentials, Event Passport,
organizer tools, and related services (together, the "Services").

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") applies, this
notice is intended to provide the information required when personal data is
collected. It should be read with the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md).

## 1. Roles and scope

The controller for the processing described in this Policy is HASHPASS
TECHNOLOGIES OÜ. For event-specific processing, an event organizer may be a
separate controller and may provide its own privacy notice. In some
business-to-business deployments, HASHPASS processes personal data for an
organizer under a data-processing agreement. The applicable event page,
agreement, or organizer notice identifies the relevant role where it differs.

This Policy does not govern independent third-party services, including
organizers, venues, payment providers, ticketing providers, wallet providers,
identity providers, social networks, and blockchain networks. Their terms and
privacy notices apply to their own processing.

## 2. Personal data we collect

Depending on how you use the Services, we may collect:

- **Account and authentication data:** name, email address, phone number when
  used for SMS authentication, account identifiers, one-time-code and
  authentication records, and identifiers supplied by an identity provider.
- **Profile and optional content:** profile information, an optional profile
  picture, and content you submit or import, such as event tickets, QR codes,
  confirmations, PDFs, invoices, images, URLs, and messages.
- **Wallet and blockchain data:** public wallet addresses, transaction or
  credential references, and other information that is publicly available on
  a blockchain. We do not request, collect, or have access to private keys or
  seed phrases.
- **Event and credential data:** registrations, passes, credentials,
  attendance and check-in records, event interactions, benefits, and related
  event information.
- **Messages:** sender and recipient identifiers and timestamps needed to
  deliver in-app messages. Content sent through supported end-to-end encrypted
  meeting chat is encrypted on your device so that HASHPASS cannot read that
  message content.
- **Technical and usage data:** device, browser, operating-system, log,
  feature-use, and security information, and information generated through
  your interactions with the Services.

You may choose not to provide certain data. However, account, authentication,
or event credential data may be necessary to provide the associated feature.

## 3. Why we use personal data

We process personal data to:

- provide, secure, maintain, and personalize the Services;
- create and authenticate accounts and deliver passes, credentials, event
  registrations, check-in, and support;
- communicate transactional, security, service, and legally required notices;
- detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, and
  misuse of credentials or APIs;
- comply with legal, regulatory, accounting, tax, and record-keeping
  obligations; and
- improve the reliability, performance, and accessibility of the Services,
  including through aggregated or appropriately anonymized analytics where
  permitted by law.

Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are the performance of a contract or
steps requested before entering one; our legitimate interests in operating,
securing, and improving the Services; compliance with legal obligations; and
your consent where required, such as for optional analytics or marketing. You
may withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing that occurred
before withdrawal.

## 4. Recipients and service providers

We do not sell personal information. We disclose data only as needed for the
purposes above, including to:

- event organizers for events for which you register or hold a credential;
- service providers acting on our behalf, including Supabase for
  authentication and database hosting, Brevo for transactional email, and
  Cloudinary for optional profile-image hosting and optimization;
- professional advisers, authorities, or other parties where required by law,
  to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a corporate transaction;
  and
- integrations that you choose to connect, subject to their own terms and
  privacy notices.

We require service providers that process data for us to handle it under
appropriate contractual and security obligations.

## 5. International transfers

Our providers and integrations may process data outside the European Economic
Area. Where a transfer is subject to GDPR transfer rules, we use an adequacy
decision, contractual safeguards, or another transfer mechanism recognized by
applicable law. Google Analytics, if enabled with your consent, is provided by
Google LLC; Google describes its applicable transfer safeguards in its
[Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 6. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described
in this Policy, including to maintain your account, provide the Services,
resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal, tax, accounting, or
fraud-prevention obligations. Routine backups may retain limited data for a
limited period before deletion. We cannot erase information stored on a public
or immutable blockchain that is outside our control.

Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months and then automatically
deleted. See [Cookies and analytics](#10-cookies-and-analytics).

## 7. Security

We use organizational and technical measures designed to protect personal
data, including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit and access controls
appropriate to the Services. Supported event meeting chat uses end-to-end
encryption based on X25519 key exchange, HKDF-SHA256, and
XChaCha20-Poly1305. No internet transmission or storage system is completely
secure; we therefore cannot guarantee absolute security.

We will notify affected parties and authorities about personal-data breaches
where required by law or contract.

## 8. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, deletion
of, restriction of, or portability of your personal data, or object to
processing. You can delete your HASHPASS account from the app. Deletion is
subject to the retention and immutable-blockchain limits described above.

To exercise a right or ask a privacy question, email
[privacy@hashpass.tech](mailto:privacy@hashpass.tech). We may ask for
information necessary to verify your identity before responding. If the GDPR
applies, you may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority;
in Estonia, this is the
[Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate](https://www.aki.ee/en).

## 9. Children

The Services may be used by minors only where lawful and with any required
parental or guardian authorization. Organizers may impose additional
age-verification or attendance requirements.

## 10. Cookies and analytics

We use essential cookies and similar technologies to provide authentication,
security, and preferences. Disabling essential cookies may prevent you from
signing in.

With consent where required, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand website
usage, including pages visited, time on page, browser and device information,
approximate city/country location, referral source, and interactions. We
configure Google Analytics with IP anonymization. You can withdraw analytics
consent through the applicable cookie controls or use Google's
[Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## 11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes to the Services, law, or our data
practices. We will publish the updated version here and revise the "Last
updated" date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain
consent.
