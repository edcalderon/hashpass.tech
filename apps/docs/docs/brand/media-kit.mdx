**SVG** is the preferred format for websites, print layouts, and large signage. It stays sharp at any size. **PNG** is useful for slides, social graphics, and editors that do not accept SVG. Wordmark PNGs are exported at 2,400 pixels wide; symbol PNGs at 1,024 pixels wide.

## For Partners

- Use the full wordmark in partner directories, sponsor rows, presentation slides, and event websites.
- Match the logo's visual height to nearby partner logos without stretching it or changing its proportions.
- Describe the relationship accurately. Use wording such as "Ticketing by HASHPASS" or "Technology partner: HASHPASS" only when it matches the agreed relationship.
- Keep event branding separate. This kit contains HASHPASS assets, not permission to reuse an event organizer's or another partner's logo.

See the [brand usage guide](./brand-guidelines.md) for backgrounds, spacing, sizing, and artwork review.

## For Media Partners and Press

Use **HASHPASS** in headlines and running text. The stylized letters belong to the artwork; do not replace letters with symbols in ordinary text. Link the first mention to [hashpass.tech](https://hashpass.tech).

### Short Description

> HASHPASS brings digital passes, event access, and attendee networking together in one platform.

### Longer Description

> HASHPASS is a platform for digital passes and event experiences. It brings passes, schedules, speaker discovery, and attendee networking into one place, helping organizers and participants manage access and stay connected around an event.

These descriptions summarize the product; they are not quotes from a spokesperson. Confirm event-specific availability, commercial terms, statistics, and announcements with your HASHPASS contact before publication.

### Before Publishing

- Use the current files from this page, not a screenshot or a logo copied from social media.
- Do not imply sponsorship, endorsement, or an official partnership that has not been agreed.
- For product screenshots, use a demo account and remove attendee names, emails, QR codes, access tokens, and private event information.
- Obtain separate permission for speaker portraits, event photography, and third-party artwork. They are not included in this kit.
- For interviews, quotes, or artwork approval, contact your existing HASHPASS representative and include your outlet, intended use, publication date, and deadline.
