# OpenProof voice-over (84 seconds)

Timings below are derived from the real synthesized narration clips
(`apps/video-studio/public/openproof/narration/en/*.mp3`, generated by
`apps/video-studio/scripts/generate-openproof-narration.mjs`), not a guessed
reading pace — scene 2's original 9s budget undershot its actual ~15s spoken
length.

**0–7s:** An event credential is only useful if it survives the platform that issued it.

**7–24s:** Today, attendance history is trapped inside private databases and closed platforms. Attendees lose access, organizers cannot verify external credentials, and partners cannot query trusted participation.

**24–35s:** HashPass OpenProof creates a shared attendance schema for events, organizers, wallets and verification applications.

**35–48s:** HashPass verifies a QR check-in, issues a signed attendance claim and transfers control of that credential to the attendee’s wallet.

**48–60s:** Arkiv makes the claim queryable, time-scoped and independently attributable. $creator proves the issuer, while $owner proves who controls the credential.

**60–71s:** Another event application can query and verify the credential without depending on HashPass’s private API.

**71–79s:** Personal data stays private. Arkiv stores only the public, verifiable credential layer.

**79–84s:** HashPass OpenProof: portable attendance for every event.
